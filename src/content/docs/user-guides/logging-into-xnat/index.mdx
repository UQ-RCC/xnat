If your organisation is part of the Australian Access Federation (AAF), you can
use your existing institutional credentials to log in.

:::note
If your organisation is not in the AAF list during sign-up, contact RCC support
for other options.
:::

## Logging in with AAF

<Steps>

1. Open [https://xnat.rcc.uq.edu.au](https://xnat.rcc.uq.edu.au)

2. Log in with the AAF Single sign-on button.

   ![XNAT AAF login page](./xnat-aaf-login-page.png)

   This will take you to your XNAT home page.

</Steps>

:::note[First time users]
You will not see any projects the first time you log in — your account has only
just been created. See [Your project](/user-guides/using-xnat/projects/your-project)
for how to get access to one.
:::
